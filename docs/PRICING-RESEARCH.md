# Budget question review — September 10, 2026

## Recommendation

The original $1,000–$5,000+ spread was credible for professional website services, and already included Under $1,000. The issue was how it could be interpreted as a starting price or a commitment before a discovery call.

Use these USD budget preferences: Under $1,000; $1,000–$2,000; $2,000–$3,500; $3,500+; Let’s discuss. Make the question optional. These are intake bands, not a published DOLPHY price list. The $3,500+ choice still accommodates a $5,000 or larger project without emphasizing that figure before scope is understood.

These sources cover different services and markets. They do not establish a single fair price or prove which form wording will convert better for DOLPHY. The revised bands and optional wording are an editorial recommendation, not a statistically validated conversion result.

## Quoting guidance for DOLPHY

Keep accepting smaller budgets, but scope those projects tightly: a focused page, supplied content and limited revisions may be more realistic than a complete custom store. For ecommerce, establish product count, variants, payment/shipping setup, content readiness, custom functionality and launch support before giving a fixed quote. Branding, photography and ongoing services should be scoped separately when requested.

Calculate the actual quote from estimated hours, your sustainable internal rate, direct costs and contingency. For illustration only, 30 hours at $50/hour is $1,500 before direct costs. Neither this example nor the intake choices establishes DOLPHY's minimum price.

## Implemented behavior

The screen asks “What budget feels comfortable? (optional)” and explains that this is a one-time website design/build budget. It explicitly says scope and price will be agreed together, with ongoing costs discussed separately. Skipping the question is saved as “Let’s discuss” in review and the admin dashboard. No service promises, preselected amount, discounts or automatic rejection are added.

Existing local drafts retain other answers. Obsolete budget selections are cleared rather than silently converted to different dollar ranges. Existing saved submissions remain unchanged.
